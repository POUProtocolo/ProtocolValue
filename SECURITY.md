# Security policy

**🇺🇸 English** | [🇧🇷 Português (Brasil)](SECURITY.pt-BR.md)


**Security reports:** pou.ultilitario@outlook.com  
Subject suggestion: `[ProtocolValue security disclosure]`

Please provide affected URL or component, impact, reproducible steps, and non-sensitive evidence. Do not send wallet seed phrases, private keys, or credentials. Do not conduct destructive tests or exploit other users' accounts. We cannot promise a response SLA or bounty program.

GitHub publication, automated checks, and site reputation reviews are **not** audits of smart contracts or assurances of investment safety.
