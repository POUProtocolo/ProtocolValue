# Glossary

**ABI:** Application Binary Interface; defines how methods and return values are encoded for contract calls.

**Block number / block hash:** Identifiers of a blockchain state and, for a hash, a particular block. A block number alone can become ambiguous across reorganizations.

**Contract-native value:** A value produced according to a smart contract's method or rules, as opposed to a quoted trade price.

**Decimals / scaling:** Factors used to convert integer on-chain units into human-readable numbers. Different tokens and methods may have different scales.

**ERC-4626:** Ethereum tokenized-vault interface that can expose conversions between shares and assets; a conversion is not necessarily a withdrawable amount.

**Indicative internal capitalization:** A contract-reference metric multiplied by a specified supply measure. It is not necessarily a tradable market capitalization or audited net asset value.

**Live on-chain reading:** A reported observation obtained by querying a selected chain state rather than solely displaying a retained historical record.

**Market price:** A price indicated by actual trading or quotes in a specified market; it can differ materially from a protocol-defined reference figure.

**Proxy contract:** A contract that may delegate execution to another implementation, potentially changing method behavior over time.

**RPC:** Remote Procedure Call; a mechanism for querying a blockchain node. RPC availability and historical-state access vary.

**Snapshot:** A retained observation for a prior block, with a timestamp and provenance where available.

**USDT denomination:** A unit of presentation or conversion based on a stated method; not proof of actual cash, USDT reserves or guaranteed one-for-one withdrawal.

**WAD:** Common fixed-point representation scaled by `10^18`. It must not be assumed for all functions.
