## What Gridinsoft said it reviewed

The response identified the following **public-facing, selected** material: catalog; About and advertising disclosures; browser interface; and selected catalog, proof and sponsorship scripts. The review noted separation between current readings and snapshots, and between reference values and market prices. Selected sponsorship code was reported to use local images and HTTPS links.

Gridinsoft stated that its review **did not identify a specific malicious payload in the inspected material**. It also stated that the previous detection was associated with the **recent registration** and not supported by a separately identified malicious object. Its support response confirmed that **no new ownership proof** was needed after previously verified domain control was linked.
