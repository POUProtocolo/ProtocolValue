## Summary
On 18 October 2026, Gridinsoft support confirmed removal of the previous “Unsettled Website” detection for both domain variants. The trust score changed from **52/100**, as referenced by the operator on 17 October, to **68/100**, as reported by Gridinsoft on 18 October. The investigation examined public site content and selected scripts. No specific malicious payload was identified in the inspected materials.
