# Contributing to ProtocolValue documentation

Thank you for helping improve the clarity and reproducibility of protocol-native data.

## Useful contributions

- Corrections to a network, contract source, method signature, formula or label.
- Reproducible discrepancies between a displayed observation and its on-chain source.
- Better definitions of live observations, snapshots, units, decimal scaling and reserve assumptions.
- Fixes to broken proof links and documentation navigation.

## What to include in an issue or pull request

Provide the asset/protocol name, network and chain ID, source contract, method signature, affected block number/hash, raw value, decoded value, RPC or explorer source, explanation and any reproducible non-sensitive evidence. Use the [protocol evidence template](docs/PROTOCOL_ENTRY_TEMPLATE.md) where helpful.

Avoid adding volatile catalog totals or exact prices to durable documentation unless the **as-of block and timestamp** are preserved.

## Security-sensitive findings

Report vulnerabilities **privately** using [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not expose exploitable findings, credentials, personal data or seed phrases in public submissions.

## Scope and permissions

This is a public **documentation repository**. The maintainers have **not published a general software/content reuse license** here. Visibility does not grant an unrestricted license to copy, redistribute or operate the project's software or documents. Contact maintainers for explicit reuse permissions before republishing substantial material.

By submitting a contribution, you confirm you have authority to share it and that it does not contain secrets or third-party material without the relevant rights. Publication decisions remain with the maintainers.
